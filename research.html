01 / ResearchArchitecture → VLSI → Silicon

One stack.
Four lenses.

We investigate specialized hardware systems for next-generation computing, spanning the full path from AI workloads to working silicon.

Approach System-level thinking

The most important opportunities live between layers.

We connect computer architecture, VLSI design, memory systems, and software instead of optimizing each layer in isolation.

Our goal is practical: higher performance and scalability with less energy and unnecessary data movement.

01

Deep Learning
Accelerators

Purpose-built neural processing engines that adapt to model structure, numerical precision, and sparsity for high-throughput, energy-efficient inference.

  • Neural processing units
  • Mixed-precision datapaths
  • Sparsity-aware architecture
02

AI
System-on-Chip

Complete digital systems that combine processors, memory, interconnects, and control for demanding AI workloads under real power and area constraints.

  • Low-power digital SoC
  • Application-specific processors
  • Scalable system architecture
03

Processing-
in-Memory

Memory-centric architectures that reduce the movement of data — one of the dominant energy and performance costs in modern AI computing.

  • In-memory processing
  • Memory-centric architecture
  • Bandwidth-efficient systems
04

Hardware–Software
Co-design

Cross-layer systems where workload insight guides architecture and hardware capability is exposed effectively through compilers and software.

  • Workload characterization
  • Compiler-aware architecture
  • Cross-layer optimization

Question What drives us
