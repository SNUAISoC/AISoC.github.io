01 Our mission

The future of AI depends on better machines, not only bigger models.

We take a holistic, system-level approach to improve performance, efficiency, and scalability — building high-performance computing platforms for future AI-driven applications.

02 Research

One stack.
Four lenses.

Our work spans the full computing stack, searching for opportunities that disappear when each layer is optimized alone.

COMPUTE / 01

Purpose-built engines for neural computation.

We explore high-throughput and energy-efficient datapaths that adapt to neural network structure, precision, and sparsity.

  • Neural processing units
  • Mixed-precision compute
  • Sparsity-aware architecture

INTEGRATE / 02

AI capability designed as a complete system.

We connect processors, memory, interconnects, and control into efficient digital systems-on-chip for real workloads.

  • Low-power digital SoC
  • Application-specific processors
  • Scalable system architecture

MOVE LESS / 03

Compute where the data already lives.

We rethink the boundary between processors and memory to reduce data movement — a dominant cost in modern AI systems.

  • In-memory processing
  • Memory-centric architecture
  • Bandwidth-efficient systems

CO-OPTIMIZE / 04

Algorithms and silicon, shaped together.

We use workload insight to guide architecture and expose hardware capability through compilers and system software.

  • Workload characterization
  • Compiler-aware architecture
  • Cross-layer optimization

Professor Park's research focuses on low-power, high-performance digital SoC design, application-specific processors, AI accelerators, and hardware–software co-design. Before joining SNU, he spent eleven years developing advanced mobile SoCs at Samsung Electronics' System LSI division.
