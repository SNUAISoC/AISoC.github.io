Jun-Seok Park, Ph.D.

AI SoC Laboratory · SNU ECE

Professor Park's work seeks new ways to make intelligent computing faster, more efficient, and more scalable — from digital circuits to complete AI systems.

Before joining Seoul National University, he spent eleven years at Samsung Electronics' System LSI division, developing advanced mobile system-on-chip technologies. His research combines that silicon experience with a system-level academic perspective.
